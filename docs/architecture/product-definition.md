# Product Definition (§1 Mission)

> Recorded in Phase 00 so every downstream phase shares the same intent.
> The controlling specification is
> [`AI_OSS_ARCHITECTURE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`](./AI_OSS_ARCHITECTURE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md).

**AI-OSS.net** is an open research-coordination platform for independent AI
researchers, open-source AI developers, safety researchers, evaluators, and
adjacent technical communities. It should feel like a public, community-run
research lab: **open like a forum, structured like a lab notebook, searchable
like a research archive, and moderated like a serious technical community.**

## The platform combines

1. **Reddit-like discussions** — posts, comments, threaded discussion, votes,
   feeds, saved posts, reports.
2. **Zones** — user-created communities (like subreddits) around topics, labs,
   projects, teams, and research directions.
3. **Realtime rooms** — text chat and voice rooms attached to zones, papers,
   posts, or ad-hoc work sessions.
4. **Research publishing** — an arXiv-like area for papers, full text, PDFs,
   code/dataset links, model cards, and versioned revisions, without platform
   vouching or peer-review gatekeeping.
5. **Community vetting** — upvotes/downvotes, comments, replication reports,
   reproducibility checklists, verification badges, safety flags, reviewer notes.
6. **Moderation & anti-abuse** — AutoModerator-inspired rule automation, mod
   queues, reports, appeals, admin enforcement, bot resistance, anti-brigading,
   transparency records.
7. **Administration** — granular global admin roles, custom permissions, mod
   tiers, audit logs, emergency controls.
8. **Governance** — community moderator selection/removal by community vote,
   protected by anti-bot and anti-brigading controls.
9. **Donations** — support for the site and development via payment infra.
10. **Modern responsive UI** — desktop and mobile, React + Tailwind,
    accessibility, dark mode, fast search/discovery.

The platform is **not** a closed proprietary lab. It emulates the coordination
capacity of a closed lab while preserving open, user-published,
community-vetted research.
